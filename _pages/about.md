I am a postdoctoral research assistant in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford, working with Patrick Rebeschini. 
Previously, I was a PhD student in the SIERRA team at INRIA and ENS in Paris, under the supervision of Umut Şimşekli. Before that I studied at Ecole Polytechnique (Paris, France) and EPFL (Lausanne, Switzerland).

My research focuses on statistical learning theory, probability theory and stochastic processes.
I am in particular interested in generalization bounds for stochastic optimization algorithms and theory for diffusion models, and implicit regularization. I also work on differential privacy, functional inequalities for Lévy processes, and variational inference.
